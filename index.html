This Month’s Guest: D.S. Palonen

Each month, Ravenloft opens its doors to an independent author from the BookTok and AuthorTok community. This month, we welcome D.S. Palonen, author of the Thrones of Achlys series.

D. S. Palonen writes adventure-forward romantasy filled with epic fantasy, fast-paced adventure, slow-burn romance, and unexpected science fiction.

The Anchor who sees and mends the magical threads binding Ravenloft.

Rowan
