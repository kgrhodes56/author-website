Meet Wayne

Wayne A. Elderkin lives in Massachusetts with his wife, Cristina, their daughter, Ava, and their loveable dog, Wicket (yes, like the Ewok).

A lifelong lover of stories in every form, Wayne draws inspiration from the music that shaped him — rock & roll, metal, and the raw edge of grunge — and from the films that fire his imagination, especially horror, supernatural thrillers, action epics, and sharp-witted comedies.

When he isn’t writing, he’s reading comics, watching movies, binge-watching shows, sitting on the beach, hitting the gym, or playing with his dog. Those everyday moments keep him grounded and shape the realms he builds, where myth and emotion meet and where characters face not only the monsters around them but also the ones within.
