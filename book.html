Book One of the Ravenloft series

Ravenloft: The Calling

After Aunt Maggie’s sudden death, Ezra, Astrid, and Symphony Hawthorne return to Ravenloft Manor, the crumbling estate they barely remember. What awaits them is a dark journey into family secrets and a past that refuses to stay buried.

As the manor begins to reveal what has long been hidden, ancient magic awakens and forces beyond the Veil begin to stir. Ravenloft is not simply calling the Hawthorne family home. It is opening the doors within them.

Every heart holds a hidden door. Ravenloft knows how to open it.
