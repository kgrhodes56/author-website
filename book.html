Book One of the Ravenloft Series

Ravenloft: The Calling

The house remembers. Even if they don’t.

After Aunt Maggie’s sudden death, Ezra, Astrid, and Symphony Hawthorne return to Ravenloft Manor, expecting only to settle an estate. Instead, they find a place filled with buried memories, family secrets, and questions that refuse to remain unanswered.

As Ravenloft begins revealing what has long been hidden, the family must decide how far they are willing to go for one another—and whether the manor has truly been waiting for them to come home.
