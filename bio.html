About K. G. Rhodes

K. G. Rhodes is a fantasy author who grew up among wild landscapes and long winters, and now writes from Washington State. Her stories lean into the haunted edges of the world, where memory, magic, and monsters collide.

She is the author of the Ravenloft series, a dark, character driven journey through mist soaked realms and the people who refuse to look away from the shadows.

When she is not writing, she is exploring backroads, reading paranormal fiction, playing MMORPGs, or plotting new ways to ruin her characters' lives on the page.
