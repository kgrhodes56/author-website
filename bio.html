Welcome

Welcome to my little corner of the world.

I'm K.G. Rhodes, author of the Ravenloft series, and I'm so glad you're here.

When I began writing Ravenloft, I wasn't simply creating a fantasy world. I was creating a place where family, mystery, courage, hope, and the unexplained could exist together.

My hope is that long after you've turned the final page, Ravenloft still feels like a place you can return to whenever you need a little mystery, hope, or courage. If readers close one of my books feeling that way, then I've accomplished exactly what I hoped.

“I believe the best fantasy stories remind us that ordinary people are capable of extraordinary courage.”
❤️

Family

Family is the heart of everything I write.

Not because families are perfect, but because they love, protect, forgive, and fight for one another when life becomes difficult. That belief lives in every page of Ravenloft.

🤝

Friends

Dreams are rarely accomplished alone.

I've been blessed with wonderful friends who have encouraged me, celebrated every milestone, and reminded me to keep believing in this journey. Their loyalty and kindness inspire many of the friendships found throughout Ravenloft.

👻

The Unexplained

I've always believed in ghosts and been fascinated by paranormal things, old houses, family legends, and the mysteries that cannot always be explained.

I believe the world still holds wonders we do not fully understand. That curiosity inspires many of the questions explored throughout Ravenloft.

I don't expect everyone to believe exactly what I do, but I hope my stories leave readers asking one simple question: What if?

☕

  • ❤️ Time with family and friends
  • 👻 Paranormal stories and mysteries

Why I Write

I write because I want readers to believe that magic is truly real.

I want them to believe that family and friends fight together and stand beside one another when darkness closes in.

Most of all, I want the characters you meet in Ravenloft to feel like people—not names on a page, but friends you cared about and missed when the story ended.
