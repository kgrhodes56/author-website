About K.G. Rhodes

K.G. Rhodes is the author of the Ravenloft series, a dark fantasy world where family, ancient magic, and buried secrets collide.

Her stories explore the hidden doors within people: courage, memory, love, power, and the truths they have not yet discovered.

When she is not writing, she enjoys paranormal fiction, creative projects, and building the world of Ravenloft for readers.
